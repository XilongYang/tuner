IndexedDB blob survival

Does a Blob stored in IndexedDB stay readable when the record around it is re-stored, the way this app re-stores a whole session on every save? Four cells, two variables: where the Blob's bytes came from, and whether the record is re-stored after being read back out of the database.

Run it in the same browser the app runs in, and leave the tab alone until it says it is done: it reloads itself several times on purpose, because a Blob read out of IndexedDB and stored again within one page life is not the case that matters. It uses its own database (blob-survival-probe) and touches nothing of the app's.
